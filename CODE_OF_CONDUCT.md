# Community code of conduct

Minutes welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-hosted discussions.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique the work and explain the reason for feedback.
- Welcome questions, acknowledge mistakes and give others room to learn.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, insult people or share their private information.
- Do not post private recordings, meeting transcripts, credentials or personal details without permission.
- Keep discussion relevant and respect a maintainer's decision to close or redirect it.

## Reporting and moderation

For an ordinary moderation concern, ask a maintainer for help in the relevant discussion without repeating harmful content. For a sensitive report, request a private contact channel without naming people or posting details publicly; a maintainer must arrange that channel first. There is no dedicated confidential reporting address published yet.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, and restrict participation when needed. They should consider context, explain decisions where appropriate, and protect the privacy of the people involved. Repeated or serious misconduct may result in exclusion from project spaces. To request reconsideration, contact a maintainer through the same channel and provide relevant context.
